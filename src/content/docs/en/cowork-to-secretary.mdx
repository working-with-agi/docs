## Two Paradigms

There are two fundamentally different approaches to AI-powered work assistance.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Cowork — Chat-Based Assistance" icon="comment">
    The user asks, the AI answers.

    - "Summarize this meeting transcript"
    - "What was decided last week?"
    - "Draft an agenda for the next meeting"

    **Human-initiated.** AI responds to what it's asked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Secretary — Autonomous Assistance" icon="rocket">
    The AI acts on its own; humans receive the results.

    - Automatically joins meetings, records and transcribes
    - Summarizes minutes, extracts action items
    - Tracks task progress, sends reminders

    **AI-initiated.** Humans focus on review and decisions.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## The Limits of Cowork

Chat-based AI assistance is powerful, but its limitations become clear as organizations grow.

| Challenge | Cowork Reality |
|---|---|
| **Information loss** | Meeting content depends on attendee memory and notes. Missed details are the norm |
| **Passive** | If the user doesn't ask the right question, the AI returns nothing |
| **Lack of context** | An AI that didn't attend the meeting has no context about the discussion |
| **Doesn't scale** | More meetings means more effort spent briefing the AI |

Cowork is a "delegate to AI" model. The delegator's bandwidth becomes the bottleneck.

---

## What Secretary Solves

Secretary is an "AI takes ownership" model.

```
Cowork:    Human → Question → AI → Answer → Human organizes
Secretary: AI → Join meeting → Record → Transcribe → Summarize → Extract actions → Human reviews
```

## Cowork + Secretary

Cowork and Secretary are not mutually exclusive. The most effective workflow is Secretary autonomously collecting and organizing information, then Cowork (chat) diving deeper into it.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              Secretary (Autonomous Layer)          │
│                                                    │
│   Join meeting → Record → Transcribe → Summarize   │
│                         │                          │
│                    KnowledgeHub                     │
│                         │                          │
├──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
│              Cowork (Interactive Layer)             │
│                         │                          │
│   "What risk did Tanaka mention last week?"         │
│   "Summarize Q3 OKR progress across projects"      │
│   "What's the background behind this decision?"    │
│                                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Secretary **gathers information**; Cowork **retrieves it**. Humans focus only on deciding what to ask.

<Aside type="tip" title="For organizations that can't hire a PMO">
Teams of 5–30 people often can't justify a dedicated PMO. Secretary takes on that role — meeting records, decision tracking, progress visibility — autonomously, powered by AI.
</Aside>
